# Preserve Highlight colours and appearances on comment-only saves

Published as [issue #127](https://github.com/ssword/Monight/issues/127),
category `bug`, state `needs-triage`, after the owner explicitly approved
publication. Found during #124.

## Reproduction

Candidate `3b26a1290c299f922a8e0a2b07b3d326c579697c`, Monight 0.6.0,
EmbedPDF 2.15.0. Create all five production Highlight colours on pages with
rotations 0 and 90; save, edit their comments in Preview, reopen in Monight,
edit only the comments and save.

Yellow `#FFCD45` becomes `#FFCD44`, Blue `#6CB4F5` becomes `#6BB4F5`, and
Purple `#B78CF2` becomes `#B78BF2`. Both page rotations reproduce. Preview's
intervening comment edits retain exact colour values, QuadPoints and decoded
appearance programs. Monight retains geometry but changes these channels and
regenerates appearances. Green and Pink remain exact in this run.

The reverse direction reproduces too: Preview-origin yellow
`[0.980392, 0.803922, 0.352941]` becomes
`[0.97647059, 0.80392158, 0.34901962]`. An Acrobat-origin Highlight's green
channel changes from `0.384308` to `0.38039216` on a Monight comment edit.

Retained PDFs and checksums are in `src-tauri/tests/fixtures/highlight-verification/`;
the [verification record](issue-124-highlight-verification.md) describes native
actions, exact candidate identity and the otherwise successful placement,
rotation and editability checks. No native stage was synthesized or repaired.

The native annotation editing adapter currently preserves comment-only
appearances for Text/Underline/StrikeOut but bypasses Highlight. Inspect the
pinned engine's colour conversion and update options before choosing a fix;
the precise root cause has not been established.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All five Monight colours retain RGB values and native appearances through
  comment-only save/reopen on pages with and without Page Rotation.
- [ ] Preview/Acrobat-origin Highlight colours and appearances survive comment
  edits, including non-integral PDF colour values.
- [ ] Genuine colour/opacity edits still produce correct appearances. Failed
  preservation blocks Save and retains unsaved edits.
- [ ] Keep QuadPoints, geometry, page content and attached replies intact; do not
  flatten output or weaken save verification.
- [ ] Repeat #124's native matrix and restore Highlight to the verified-tools
  list only after the failure is resolved.

New Highlight creation is suspended by the #124 verification change. Existing
Highlights remain visible and are preserved when other admitted annotations are
saved; their editing interaction is locked.
