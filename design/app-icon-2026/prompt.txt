Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: a finished, premium macOS app icon visual concept for Monight (墨页), a PDF reader centered on comfortable night reading and remembering the reader's place.
Primary request: Design one exceptionally refined Apple Liquid Glass style app icon, using the current 2026 visual direction: simple strong silhouettes, subtly layered materials, crisp controlled specular edges with light from above, restrained refraction, calm optical depth. The concept is “a page of moonlight”.
Composition: one centered rounded-square macOS icon tile, straight-on orthographic view, no perspective tilt. Tile occupies 88% of the square image width and height, with an Apple-like continuous superellipse corner profile. Outside the tile is genuinely transparent alpha. No surrounding scene or presentation board. Inside the tile, the main symbol fills about 66% of tile width and 70% of tile height. Generous, optically balanced negative space.
Backdrop: a rich ink-blue tile with a quiet near-black-navy to midnight-blue gradient, slightly lighter at the top; subtly luminous but not neon. It should look elegant beside Apple's native Mac app icons.
Subject: a broad upright document page made of luminous moon-white frosted glass with softly rounded lower corners and a clear, simple folded upper-right corner. A second subtly offset translucent blue page behind it communicates an ongoing reading session, visible as a narrow silhouette on the lower-left. Two broad, short, low-contrast blue-gray horizontal reading marks in the upper-left part of the main page, generous spacing, only enough to signify a PDF. In the upper-right quadrant, a bold warm ivory crescent moon sits slightly in front of and overlaps the main page, close to its folded corner. Moon is a real filled crescent silhouette opening to the upper right, with a thick curved body, NOT a thin ring, not a circle with an outline. Its warm ivory body and delicate champagne rim contrast clearly against the cool document. Crescent is about 29% of the tile width. These elements form one cohesive compact emblem.
Materials: a milky translucent document surface, smooth delicate bevels, sharp but quiet rim light, soft shallow interlayer shadows; warmth comes only from the moon, cool color from the ink-blue background. Preserve enough opacity in the page for instant recognition at 32px. Carefully crafted optical glass, not a plastic toy or chrome sculpture. No heavy extrusion. No exaggerated transparency.
Palette: ink navy #101B30 and #1A3150, moon-white #F3F5F2, a trace of the app's blue #4A9EFF in the rear page, restrained warm ivory #FFF0CD in the crescent. No purple or rainbow.
Text: none. No letters, no wordmark, no PDF label, no numerals.
Avoid: stars, sparkles, night landscapes, sun, pen, extra badges, particles, neon bloom, noisy gradients, thick enclosing outlines, floating UI panels, Apple logos, stock icon clipart, watermarks, mockup captions, alternate variations, grid layouts. Exactly one icon.
Output: a high-resolution square PNG, ideally 1024x1024, with real alpha outside the rounded-square icon, ready for visual review as a static macOS icon design.
